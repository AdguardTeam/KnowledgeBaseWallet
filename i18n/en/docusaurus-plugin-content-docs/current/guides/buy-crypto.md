AdGuard Wallet lets you buy and sell cryptocurrency directly in the app through third-party providers. You can buy crypto with a credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, or bank transfer — and sell crypto back to fiat currency when you need to.

<!-- screenshot: Buy & Sell tab in navigation — showing Buy crypto / Sell crypto tabs -->

Depending on your country and the provider, you may need to complete a KYC (identity verification) process for certain transactions. This is handled by the provider, not by AdGuard Wallet.

## How to buy crypto

1. Open AdGuard Wallet and go to the _Buy & Sell_ screen.
2. Make sure the _Buy crypto_ tab is selected.
3. Choose the currency you want to spend (fiat currency) and the cryptocurrency you want to receive.
4. Enter the amount — you can type it in either the fiat or crypto field; the other field will update automatically.
5. Review the exchange rate and the provider fee.
6. Tap _Continue_ — the app will redirect you to the provider’s page in your browser.
7. Follow the provider’s instructions to complete the payment. You may need to verify your identity (KYC).

After the transaction is completed, you will be redirected back to the app. The purchased crypto will appear in your wallet.

<!-- screenshot: Buy crypto screen — currency selection, amount input, exchange rate, and Continue button -->

## How to sell crypto

1. Open AdGuard Wallet and go to the _Buy & Sell_ screen.
2. Select the _Sell crypto_ tab.
3. Choose the cryptocurrency you want to sell and the fiat currency you want to receive.
4. Enter the amount.
5. Review the exchange rate and the provider fee.
6. Tap _Continue_ — the app will redirect you to the provider’s page in your browser.
7. Follow the provider’s instructions to send your crypto and receive fiat.

After the transaction is completed, you will be redirected back to the app.

<!-- screenshot: Sell crypto screen — similar layout with opposite currency direction -->

:::note

Buying and selling crypto is provided by third-party partners (such as Guardarian), not by AdGuard Wallet. The provider handles all payments, KYC verification, and transaction execution.

:::

## Supported methods and limits

- **Payment methods** — credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, bank transfer (varies by provider and country)
- **Supported assets** — Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Tether, USDC, and thousands of other crypto assets
- **Fiat currencies** — USD, EUR, and other major currencies (varies by provider and country)
- **Limits** — minimum and maximum amounts apply per currency pair. The app will show these limits when you select a pair.

If buy and sell are not available in your country, you will see a notice in the app.

## What if something goes wrong

- **Country not supported** — buy and sell may not be available in your region
- **KYC not passed** — check the provider’s requirements and try again
- **Transaction failed** — the provider will show the reason. If the issue persists, [contact support](mailto:support@adguard-wallet.com)
- **Funds not received** — check the transaction status. If the transaction shows as completed but the funds are not in your wallet, refresh the balance or contact support
