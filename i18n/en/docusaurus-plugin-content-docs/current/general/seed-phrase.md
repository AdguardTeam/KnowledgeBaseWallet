A **seed phrase** (also called a recovery phrase) is a unique set of 12, 18, or 24 words that gives you access to your cryptocurrency wallet and funds. Think of it as the master key to your wallet — anyone who has it can control your assets, and if you lose it, no one can help you recover it.

<!-- screenshot: Seed phrase displayed during wallet creation — 12 words with copy and backup options -->

Your seed phrase is generated by your wallet when you create it and is the only way to restore access to your funds. You can use the same seed phrase in AdGuard Wallet and other compatible wallets — it’s not locked to any single app.

## Why it’s important

In a non-custodial wallet like AdGuard Wallet, your funds live on the blockchain, not in the app. The seed phrase is the only thing that connects you to those funds. This means:

- **If someone steals your seed phrase**, they have full control over your crypto. They can transfer it to their own wallet, and you won’t be able to get it back.
- **If you lose your seed phrase**, you lose access to your funds permanently. There is no “forgot my password” option, no customer support that can reset it — by design.

:::warning

Never share your seed phrase with anyone. AdGuard Wallet support will never ask for it. If someone asks for your seed phrase — even if they claim to be support — they are trying to steal your funds.

:::

## How AdGuard Wallet protects your seed phrase

AdGuard Wallet stores your seed phrase securely on your device:

- **Encrypted locally** — your seed phrase is encrypted and saved directly on your device, not on any external server.
- **Protected by password and biometrics** — every transaction requires authentication, and your seed phrase is only decrypted when needed.
- **Secure storage** — the app uses hardware-backed security features specific to your platform (TPM on Windows, Secure Enclave on iOS and macOS, hardware-backed Keystore on Android) and follows the BIP39 industry standard for mnemonic generation and validation.

<!-- screenshot: Security settings screen — showing password and biometrics toggles -->

Because AdGuard Wallet is non-custodial, your seed phrase never leaves your device. Not even AdGuard can access it.

## How to store your seed phrase safely

Your seed phrase is the most important piece of information you’ll ever have in crypto. Store it carefully.

<!-- screenshot: Cloud backup option screen — iCloud / Google Drive selection -->

**Do:**

- Write it down on paper and keep it in a safe place (a fireproof safe, a safety deposit box).
- Use the cloud backup option in AdGuard Wallet if you prefer a digital copy — it’s encrypted.
- Consider additional local backups (Keychain/Passwords on your device).
- Store multiple copies in different secure locations.

**Don’t:**

- Store it in plain text on your phone, computer, or in a note-taking app.
- Take a screenshot of it or save it in an email draft.
- Share it with anyone, including people claiming to be support staff.
- Enter it on any website or app you don’t fully trust.
