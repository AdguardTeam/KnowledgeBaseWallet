AdGuard Wallet is designed with privacy as a core principle. As a non-custodial wallet, your wallet information stays on your device. Transaction history is fetched with the help of third-party blockchain data providers and may be stored on our servers and their servers to display it to you in the app, but it is not linked to your personal identity.

## What data we collect and what we never collect

AdGuard Wallet does not collect personal data by default. The app does not collect:

- Your **balances** or transaction history (fetched via third-party blockchain data providers)
- Any **personal information** (name, email, phone number) unless you contact support

Your **wallet addresses** are stored to provide the app functionality — without them your balances and transaction history could not be displayed. Your **seed phrase** and private keys are encrypted and stored on your device. The app needs to access them to sign transactions — they never leave your device and are not sent to any server.

You are the sole owner of your funds and your information.

## Telemetry and error reporting

You have the option to help us improve AdGuard Wallet by sharing **anonymized technical data**. This is entirely optional and off by default:

- **Telemetry** — anonymized usage data that helps us understand how the app is used
- **Error reports** — technical information when something goes wrong, so we can fix it

If you choose to enable this, the data is sent using **OHTTP (Oblivious HTTP)** — a privacy protocol that ensures no single party can see both who sent the data and what was sent.

:::note

Telemetry and error reporting are always opt-in. You can enable or disable them at any time in the app settings.

:::

## Contacting support

If you contact AdGuard Wallet support, we may ask for information related to your question, such as your email address and details about the issue. This helps us resolve your issue faster. Your email is stored for the duration of the support request. The support team will **never** ask for your seed phrase, passwords, or private keys.

:::warning

Anyone who asks for your seed phrase — even if they claim to be support — is trying to steal your funds. AdGuard Wallet support will never request it.

:::

For full details, see the [AdGuard Wallet Privacy Policy](https://adguard-wallet.com/en/privacy.html).
