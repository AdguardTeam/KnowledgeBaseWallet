Creating a wallet in AdGuard Wallet generates a unique seed phrase (12, 18, or 24 words) that gives you access to your funds. This seed phrase is the key to your wallet — keep it safe and never share it with anyone.

First [install AdGuard Wallet](/guides/install-and-setup), then have a safe place ready to store your seed phrase.

## Creating a wallet

1. Open AdGuard Wallet and tap *Create wallet*.
1. Follow the onboarding steps until your seed phrase is displayed.
1. **Write down your seed phrase** and store it in a safe place.
1. Choose how to back up your seed phrase: iCloud, Google Drive, or manual.
1. Confirm your seed phrase by selecting 4 words from it.

Password and biometric authentication are part of the initial setup described in [How to install and set up AdGuard Wallet](/guides/install-and-setup).

Once your wallet is created, you can send and receive crypto, swap tokens, and manage multiple wallets.

For detailed instructions, see [What is a seed phrase](/general/seed-phrase).
