sidebar_position: 2
---

Creating a wallet in AdGuard Wallet generates a unique seed phrase (12, 18, or 24 words) that gives you access to your funds. This seed phrase is the key to your wallet — keep it safe and never share it with anyone.

1. Open AdGuard Wallet and tap *Create wallet*.
    ![Onboarding *mobile](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/wallet/guides/onboarding.png)
1. Create a strong password and name your wallet.
    ![Creating wallet](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/wallet/guides/create_wallet.png)
1. Your seed phrase will be displayed. Choose how to back up your seed phrase: iCloud, Google Drive, or manual (write it down).
    ![Back up seed phrase](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/wallet/guides/seed_phrase_password.png)
1. Confirm your seed phrase by selecting 4 words from it.
    ![Back up seed phrase *mobile](https://cdn.adtidy.org/content/kb/wallet/guides/remember_seed_phrase.png)
1. Optionally enable **biometric authentication** for faster access.

Once your wallet is set up, you can send and receive crypto, swap tokens, and manage multiple wallets.
