Creating a wallet in AdGuard Wallet generates a unique seed phrase (12, 18, or 24 words) that gives you access to your funds. This seed phrase is the key to your wallet — keep it safe and never share it with anyone.

Make sure AdGuard Wallet is [installed](/guides/install-and-setup), and have a safe place ready to store your seed phrase.

## Creating a wallet

1. Open AdGuard Wallet and tap *Create wallet*.
1. Follow the onboarding steps — they explain what a seed phrase is and why it matters.
1. Create a strong password. This password protects your seed phrase on your device.
1. Your seed phrase will be displayed. **Write it down** and store it in a safe place.
1. Choose how to back up your seed phrase: iCloud, Google Drive, or manual (write it down).
1. Confirm your seed phrase by selecting 4 words from it.
1. Optionally enable **biometric authentication** for faster access.

Once your wallet is set up, you can send and receive crypto, swap tokens, and manage multiple wallets.

For detailed instructions, see [What is a seed phrase](/general/seed-phrase).
